Support

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Questions, bug reports, or account help: totallynotwoojin@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

  • I did not get a sign-in code. Codes come by email from Gist; check spam, then tap Resend code. Codes expire after a few minutes.
  • How do I turn translation on or off? Open a conversation, tap Conversation settings, and choose the translation pair or turn it off. Originals are always shown.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, messages you sent, and device registrations.
  • Someone is bothering me. Long-press a message to report it, or open the person's profile to block them.
