Newone Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Newone is a messaging application for iOS, Android, and the web. This policy explains what information Newone collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address, chosen username, display name, and optional profile details (avatar, status message, preferred language). Your email address is used to sign you in with one-time codes and is never shown to other users.
  • Messages and content. Messages, reactions, and attachments you send are stored on our servers so they can be delivered to your conversations and synchronized across your devices.
  • Connections. Your contact connections, message requests, and blocks.
  • Device and security information. Device registration for push notifications, session and installation identifiers, and security event records used to protect your account.

Automatic translation

Newone can automatically translate messages between English, Spanish, and Korean. When translation is enabled, message text is processed by our AI translation provider under a zero-data-retention configuration: the provider is contractually restricted from storing or training on your messages. You can turn translation off per conversation in the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not show advertising and do not share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not read your conversations except as required to operate the service (for example, automated safety processing of reported content) or as required by law.

Who can see what

Other users can find you by your exact username or a close prefix of it. Your profile is visible to your connections and to members of conversations you join. There is no public directory of users. Anyone can send you a message request; you decide whether to accept, decline, or block.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. Deletion permanently disables sign-in, removes your profile information and username from circulation, and detaches your identity from the account. Messages you already sent remain in recipients’ conversations, attributed to “Deleted account.” Backup copies are removed on a rolling schedule.

Where data is processed

Newone is hosted on Supabase infrastructure in the United States. Translation processing uses OpenRouter with pinned, zero-retention model routing.

Children

Newone is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and note the new effective date above.
