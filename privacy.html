Gist

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Gist ("Gist", "we") is a messaging app with built-in translation. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you control.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (used for sign-in codes), username, display name, optional profile photo, and preferred language.
  • Messages and attachments: texts, replies, reactions, photos, videos, voice messages, and files you send. They are stored on our servers so they can be delivered to the people you share them with. Gist does not use end-to-end encryption; messages are encrypted in transit and at rest on our infrastructure.
  • Contacts you choose: people you search for, message, connect with, or block inside Gist. We never read your phone's address book.
  • Device and app data: an app installation identifier, device platform and app version, session information, locale, and, if you enable notifications, a push token (stored encrypted).
  • Diagnostics: error identifiers, latency and delivery status. Diagnostics never contain message text.

Translation and AI features

When a conversation has a translation pair, or when you ask for a translation, summary, or language detection, the text of the relevant messages is sent to a language-model provider (OpenRouter, routed to Google Vertex AI) under a zero-data-retention configuration. Originals are always kept unchanged, and you can turn translation off per conversation.

How we use data

  • To deliver messages, notifications, and translations.
  • To keep accounts secure: sign-in codes, session and device management, abuse prevention, and rate limiting.
  • To handle reports of abuse or translation quality problems that you submit.

We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising.

Sharing

Data is processed by our infrastructure providers: Supabase (hosting, database, storage), Expo and Apple/Google (push notifications), Resend (sign-in emails), and the language-model provider named above. Each receives only what is needed for its function.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete a message for everyone, hide it for yourself, remove your profile photo, revoke device sessions, and delete your account from Settings. Account deletion removes your profile, messages you sent, attachments, and device registrations; anonymised diagnostics may remain.

Children

Gist is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data from the app or by contacting us. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws; contact us to exercise them.
